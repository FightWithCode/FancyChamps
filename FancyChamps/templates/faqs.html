Registration on FancyChamps
1. What is FancyChamps?

Ans : FancyChamps is one of the Best fantasy sports platforms in India. FancyChamps help the users to earn real money by using their skills and knowledge of sports.

2. How do I sign up on FancyChamps?

Ans : It's easy! Once you are on FancyChamps homepage, fill up the short registration form, get the bonus and start earning!

3. Why can't I log in to my Account?

Ans : Opps! Please check whether your registered e-mail address and password are entered correctly and try again. If you've forgotten your password, click on 'Forgot Password', enter your registered email ID and we’ll send you a link shortly to reset your password via e-mail. If you've forgotten your registered email id, reach out to us via our Contact Us page.

4. Can I have multiple accounts on FancyChamps?

Ans : No! A user is allowed to have only one account with valid documents on FancyChamps. Please refer T&C for further details.

5. Can I update / edit my information?

Ans : Why not! Simply log into FancyChamps and click on the top right of the page then select 'My Profile' and then edit your informations. You will be able to update/edit your full name and DOB.

6. I did not get any confirmation e-mail after I signed up. What to do?

Ans : There is a possibility that the e-mail could have been marked as ‘Spam’. Henceforth, we suggest you to check your 'Spam Flolder' and mark the e-mail from FancyChamps as ‘Not Spam’.The other reason can be, you may not have entered the correct e-mail address during registration. If the issue still persists, get in touch with us via our Contact Us page.

7. I don't know much about fantasy cricket – can I still play?

Ans : The fantasy cricket services offered by FancyChamps are for everyone including those who don’t know much about cricket. So, yes, you can play even if you don’t know much about cricket. In addition, the “How to Play” section of FancyChamps website can assist you in playing the game by briefing you about the playing tactics.

Playing, Scoring and Points on FancyChamps
1. When does Fantasy Cricket start on FancyChamps?

Ans : Fantasy Cricket on FancyChamps starts as soon as the match starts. Usualy, seconds before the first bowl is bowled in the match.

2. Can I choose players from one side only?

Ans : No, the users cannot choose players from one team only. They are allowed to choose a maximum of 7 players from a single side and the remaining players should be selected from the other side.

3. Can I edit my team during a round/match?

Ans : No! Once the round/match is started, users are not allowed to change/create any of their teams. Users can only change/create teams before the match starts.

4. Can I choose which of my teams will compete in a contest (Team 1 or 2) after joining?

Ans : Not yet, But our team is working hard to make this happend. You will see this feature soon!

5. How will I score points?

Ans : You will score points depending on how the players in your team perform in the live match. So, make sure you pick a winning combination to help you win! Check out our ‘Fantasy Points System’ for more information.

6. When do the scores get updated?

Ans : Points are usually updated every over! The final points including your team's total and your rank are updated and finalized once the match is over. Winners are declared and Winnings are distributed only after our team verifies the points.

7. What happens when there is a tie between users? How will you distribute the prize?

Ans : When there is a tie between 2 or more users, the prize money is divided equally between the users.

8. Do I get points for a substituted players?

Ans : No, your team is not awarded any points for a substitute player in case he replaces any player on-field as the substitute is not considered as a part of playing XI.

9. Do I get points for a Super Over in a Match?

Ans : We do not consider the Super Over points. Because the runs/wickets scored by the players are not considered in their official records.

Contest and Prizes on FancyChamps
1. How do I join contests?

Ans : To join contests, click on the ‘Join Now’ button below the specific contest you wish to join.

2. What are the Cash Contest

Ans : The cash contest organized by FancyChamps are contests wherein the users can participate by paying a pre-defined entry fee and can compete with other users in order to win prizes and real money.

3. How many winners will be announced for contests?

Ans : The winners will vary for different contests. The number of winners and their prizes in a contest will be shown by clicking on the '₹ Winnings ' link and this information will be disclosed as soon as the contest is made available to join.

4. What happens if there is a tie between winners of a multiple winner contest?

Ans : In case of a tie between winners of a multiple winner contest for any position, the prize money for the winner's position and the immediately next winning position (if any), and so forth in accordance with the number of tied winners, will be aggregated and shared equally among the tied winners. All other prize money will remain as per the winner's standing.

5. What happens if an ODI/T20 match is abandoned?

Ans : If the real match is abandoned fantasy match will be cancelled and the entry fees will be refunded. This includes scenarios where the real match is abandoned without a single ball being bowled, as well as where the match is abandoned after it has started.

6. What do I win in a cash contest?

Ans : The users will win real cash prizes, if they are playing in a cash contest.

7. How do you decide the winner of a contest?

Ans : The winner in a contest is decided on the basis of the aggregate a team scores. The user whose team scores the highest aggregate of scores on the basis of the on-field performance of their chosen players is announced as the winner of a contest.
